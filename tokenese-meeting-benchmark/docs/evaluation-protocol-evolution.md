# Evolution experiment protocol — frozen before API evaluation

Reference: `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14`. The answer instruction and Answer schema are identical for all primary methods. Grammar legends, examples, gold labels, and repair history are never passed to the answer model. The compiler receives facts and a data-only grammar, not questions. Literal fields, order, and duplicates must round-trip exactly.

Search may read development data only. Keep eight anchors and rotate eight development failures/contrasts; compare candidates only on identical question sets. Six generations, six evaluated candidates per generation, 16 questions per candidate; up to 256 local candidates per generation. Counterexamples are development-only. Repairs change one structural rule, require paired improvement without regression, and remain unconfirmed unless verified on two source meetings. Up to four finalists see full development and its archive. At most two frozen finalists see validation, one winner sees test. No held-out-driven tuning.

All paid research commands require `--run-api`. One persistent SQLite ledger covers annotation, extraction, probes, baselines, and qualification. Default ceilings: 3,500 actual calls / $10 total, at most 1,500 discovery calls. Reserve remaining qualification allowance before searching. Charge failures; unknown usage is incomplete accounting, not zero. Replays preserve original usage and are not new provider cache hits. Input/cached-input/output and extraction/research costs are separate. Interrupted dispatches remain reserved. Provider retries are disabled.

Required baselines: raw excerpts, explicit prose facts, compact English facts, best initial seed, evolved facts. Compare gold and parsed facts separately. Record actual API input and output tokens, full visible prompt estimates, tokenizer version, model, schema/prompt hashes, provenance and dataset hashes. Batch comparisons are separate development ablations with four questions and a shared structured schema. Report recurring workflow cost at 1/4/10 independent questions with extraction charged once; batch results must not be mixed into that accounting.

Both validation and test require 12 source-disjoint meetings / 120 questions, at least 119 exact correct answers, zero new critical errors relative to either English baseline, zero integrity failures, lower total actual answer-input tokens than both English baselines, and independently reviewed annotations for language qualification. Product qualification additionally requires the parsed route to meet accuracy and no-new-critical-error gates relative to raw/parsed-English and an actually cheaper measured workflow. Missing/invalid answers and wrong abstentions fail. Exact value and `found` must agree. Product and language statuses are separate; insufficient data/review/accounting is `incomplete_evaluation`, not success. Public model-drafted annotations remain provisional even when source quotes match. Unsupported content must fall back to raw notes.

Saved artifacts include public examples only. User-pasted notes, exact prompts, extraction, and answers remain in process/session memory. Product mode requires a compatible qualified frozen manifest; an explicit research comparison can inspect an unqualified grammar with a visible warning. No synthetic success animation or undocumented independent review.

Boundaries: this experiment preserves five meeting-fact kinds, not all possible prose. It does not train LLM weights or claim general losslessness. The iterative loop changes language rules using development feedback. Historical V1 data is diagnostic development data, never independent held-out evidence.

## Suite audit amendment (before validation was opened)

The first development run showed that model-authored source questions included unsupported background/status facts and occasional label-format errors. Preserve those original questions as `holistic_questions`; do not silently relabel their failures. Add ten deterministic field-preservation questions per public source in `questions`, derived from the annotated facts without answer-model outputs. These isolate the language experiment. The broader original questions drive the parsed product evaluation and raw fallback comparison. Both tracks remain provisional until independent semantic review. Twelve documents per split are source-disjoint excerpts, not necessarily twelve individual meeting sessions: some published council updates summarize multiple sessions. Cross-series splits also introduce domain shift.

The dataset has 36 public source excerpts, 360 bounded-language questions, 360 broader source questions, and 40 development-only synthetic contrast questions. The fixed pilot has eight public anchors and eight paired contrast questions. Exact grading preserves Unicode, signed values, and significant internal symbols; case, whitespace, and a terminal period are tolerated. `found` must still match. Report category/macro-source results, incorrect abstentions, unsupported positive answers, invalid outputs, paired regressions, extraction multiset precision/recall/F1, and descriptive uncertainty intervals. Broader source labels and gold facts are not upgraded to reviewed truth by these mechanical checks.

The repair/no-repair ablation uses the first two matched rounds (192 logical probes in each arm, identical question IDs per round). Later adaptive development and the final full-suite candidates are outside that matched comparison. Research costs include earlier development runs and failed annotation drafts. Selection may freeze a failing finalist for a diagnostic test only; it must retain `incomplete_evaluation` or `no_qualified_candidate` and cannot become a production route without all gates.
